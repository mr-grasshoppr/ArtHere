ART HERE PORTLAND
PDX Community Survey — Design Document
Last updated: July 6, 2026

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The PDX Community Survey is a multi-step form at artishere.org/survey. It
collects information about Portland-area residents, their awareness of local
artists, and—for artists—details about their practice and goals. Results are
shared publicly with the community and used to shape Art Here's programming.

Platform:         artishere.org/survey (Next.js, mobile-responsive)
Response storage: PostgreSQL via Neon / Vercel Postgres
Draft saving:     Responses are saved to the database after each step
                  (partial drafts are captured)
Raffle:           One winner randomly selected weekly through September 15, 2026.
                  $25 gift card to a local Portland business.


================================================================================
INTRO PAGE
================================================================================

Shown before the first survey step. Disappears once the respondent advances
past Step 1.

--- Intro copy ---

Art Here is a community initiative to celebrate local artists and connect them
to the residents, businesses, and organizations around them.

The purpose of this survey is to understand how to better support the arts in
Portland, OR. We'll share findings publicly with the community and with our
partners. Whether you're an artist, a neighbor, or just curious, we'd love to
hear from you. Everyone who completes the survey is eligible to win a $25 gift
card to a local shop that supports Portland artists.

--- Disclaimer ---

By completing this survey, you agree to the use of your responses to support
the arts and artists in Portland. Any quotes or data shared with our partners
will not be tied to your name or personal information. You will have the option
to respond completely anonymously if you choose not to share your email or
participate in the raffle.


================================================================================
SURVEY FLOW
================================================================================

The survey has 9–11 screens depending on the respondent's answers. Two branches
are driven by the "Do you make art?" question (Step 5).

PATH A — Artist (primary occupation or active practice):
  Step 1:  Location
  Step 2:  About You — Occupation
  Step 3:  About Portland — Familiarity
  Step 4:  About Portland — Detail
  Step 5:  About You — Do you make art?
  Step 6:  About You — Career Stage  [shown only for primary/active artists]
  Step 7:  About Your Art — Recent Activities
  Step 8:  About Your Art — Goals & Support
  Step 9:  Get Involved
  Step 10: Email
  Step 11: One Last Thing (referral)

PATH B — Non-artist (student, hobby, or "No"):
  Step 1:  Location
  Step 2:  About You — Occupation
  Step 3:  About Portland — Familiarity
  Step 4:  About Portland — Detail
  Step 5:  About You — Do you make art?
           → If student/hobby/Other: skip Career Stage, skip Practice screens
           → If "No": skip Career Stage and all Practice screens
  Step 6:  Get Involved
  Step 7:  Email
  Step 8:  One Last Thing (referral)


================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP QUESTION REFERENCE
================================================================================

Each step occupies its own screen. Respondents can navigate back to previous
steps. Progress is shown as a thin bar at the top of the form.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Location (Where Are You Based?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. What is your zip code?
    Type: Text input — numeric only, max 5 digits
    Required. Must be exactly 5 digits to proceed.
    No validation against a list of accepted zip codes (any 5-digit code accepted).
    Field accepts numerals only; non-numeric input is stripped automatically.

Q2. What neighborhood(s) do you consider yourself part of?
    Type: Text input — open text
    Hint: "Whether you live, work, or spend time there."
    Required. Must be non-empty to proceed.
    Placeholder: "e.g. Multnomah Village, St. Johns, Hawthorne…"

C1. I am 18 years of age or older.
    Type: Checkbox — required
    Required. Must be checked to proceed.
    This survey is for adults only (18+).

C2. I understand my responses will not be tied to my name or personal
    information.
    Type: Checkbox — required
    Required. Must be checked to proceed.
    Both checkboxes (C1 and C2) must be checked along with Q1 and Q2 before
    the Next button activates.

[Raffle footnote displayed at bottom of Step 1:]
No purchase necessary. Open to US residents 18 and older. One winner randomly
selected weekly through September 15, 2026. Winners receive a $25 gift card to
a local business that supports Portland artists. Notified by email.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — About You (Occupation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q3. What field do you work in?
    Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select all that apply
    Hint: "If not currently working, what field did you most recently work in?
          Select all that apply."
    Required: at least one option must be selected to proceed.

    Options (first 10 randomized; last 5 always pinned at bottom):
      Arts (Visual Art, Dance, Music, Theater)
      Business or Professional Services
      Design or Creative Services
      Education
      Federal or State Government
      Healthcare
      Local Government (City or County)
      Non-profit
      Technology
      Trades or Manufacturing
      Homemaker  [pinned]
      Not currently working  [pinned]
      Retired  [pinned]
      Other  [pinned] → reveals text input
      Prefer not to say  [pinned, exclusive]

    Notes:
    - The first 10 options are randomized in order on each visit.
    - "Prefer not to say" is exclusive: selecting it clears all other
      selections, and selecting any other option clears it.
    - Selecting "Other" reveals a text input for free-text description.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — About Portland (Familiarity)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q4. How aware are you of local artists in your area?
    Type: Single-select (radio)
    Required. One option must be selected to proceed.

    Options:
      Very aware
      Somewhat aware
      A little aware
      Not at all aware


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — About Portland (Detail)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q5. In your opinion, what people, places, or organizations most help artists
    thrive in Portland?
    Type: Textarea — open text
    Hint: "Name one to three."
    Optional. Not required to proceed.

Q6. How, if at all, would you like to connect with or support the artist
    community in Portland?
    Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select all that apply
    Hint: "Select all that apply."
    Required: at least one option must be selected to proceed.

    Options (first 7 randomized; last 2 pinned):
      Purchase artwork  [randomized]
      Hire or commission artwork  [randomized]
      Collaborate with artists  [randomized]
      Attend events  [randomized]
      Visit art galleries or shows  [randomized]
      Share artists' work  [randomized]
      Volunteer  [randomized]
      Other  [pinned] → reveals text input
      None of the above  [pinned, exclusive]

    Notes:
    - "None of the above" is exclusive: selecting it clears all other selections.
    - Selecting "Other" reveals a text input for free-text description.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — About You (Artist Status)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q7. Do you make art?
    Type: Single-select (radio)
    Required. One option must be selected to proceed.
    This answer drives the branching logic for Steps 6–8 (see Skip Logic).

    Options:
      Yes, it is my primary occupation
      Yes, I have an active art practice alongside other work
      Yes, I'm an art student
      Yes, for fun or as a hobby
      No
      Other → reveals text input

    Notes:
    - Selecting "Other" reveals a text input ("Tell us more").
      The text input must be non-empty to proceed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — About You (Career Stage) — CONDITIONAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SHOWN IF:   Q7 = "Yes, it is my primary occupation"
            OR "Yes, I have an active art practice alongside other work"
SKIPPED IF: Any other answer to Q7 (student, hobby, No, Other)

Q8. How long have you been practicing as an artist?
    Type: Single-select (radio)
    Required. One option must be selected to proceed.

    Options:
      Just getting started (less than 2 years)
      Early career (2–5 years)
      Established (5–15 years)
      Longtime (15+ years)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 — About Your Art (Recent Activities) — CONDITIONAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SHOWN IF:   Q7 = anything other than "No" (respondent is making art in any form)
SKIPPED IF: Q7 = "No"

Q9. In the past year, which of the following have you done in support of
    your art?
    Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select all that apply
    Hint: "Select all that apply."
    Required: at least one option must be selected to proceed.

    Options (first 7 randomized; last 2 pinned):
      Sold original artwork  [randomized]
      Taken commissions for my artwork  [randomized]
      Shown my artwork in galleries, shows, or public events  [randomized]
      Applied for an artist grant or residency  [randomized]
      Received an artist grant or residency  [randomized]
      Collaborated with other artists or organizations  [randomized]
      Taken a class or training to support my art practice  [randomized]
      Other  [pinned] → reveals text input
      None of the above  [pinned, exclusive]

    Notes:
    - "None of the above" is exclusive: selecting it clears all other selections.
    - Selecting "Other" reveals a text input for free-text description.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 — About Your Art (Goals & Support) — CONDITIONAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SHOWN IF:   Q7 = anything other than "No"
SKIPPED IF: Q7 = "No"

Q10. What are your current goals as an artist?
     Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select up to 3
     Required: at least one option must be selected to proceed.

     Options (all randomized except "Other" which is pinned):
       Sell my artwork  [randomized]
       Find more commissions  [randomized]
       Share my artwork with more people  [randomized]
       Connect with other local artists  [randomized]
       Show my artwork in galleries, shows, or public events  [randomized]
       Find studio space or places to make my work  [randomized]
       Receive an artist grant or residency  [randomized]
       Collaborate with other artists  [randomized]
       Find classes or training to support my art practice  [randomized]
       Other  [pinned] → reveals text input

     Notes:
     - Maximum 3 selections. Once 3 are selected, remaining options are
       visually disabled.
     - Selecting "Other" reveals a text input ("Tell us more"). The text input
       must be non-empty if Other is selected.

Q11. What local places, organizations, institutions, or businesses have been
     most important to supporting you and your art practice?
     Type: Textarea — open text
     Hint: "Name one to three."
     Required. Must be non-empty to proceed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9 — Get Involved
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q12. In what ways, if any, would you like to get involved with Art Here?
     Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select all that apply
     Hint: "Select all that apply."
     Required: at least one option must be selected to proceed.

     Options (fixed order — NOT randomized):
       Keep me posted on Art Here news
       Become a featured artist
       Volunteer to help Art Here
       Join the parade at Multnomah Days 2026 (August 15, Portland)
       Partner or collaborate
       Other → reveals text input
       None of the above  [exclusive]

     Notes:
     - "None of the above" is exclusive: selecting it clears all other selections.
     - Selecting "Become a featured artist" + providing an email triggers an
       automatic magic link to set up an artist profile.

Q13. Would you like to be entered in a raffle for completing this survey?
     Type: Single-select (radio)
     Hint: "Raffle winners will receive a $25 gift card to a local shop that
           supports Portland artists."
     Required. One option must be selected to proceed.
     Answering "Yes" makes the email address field required on the next step.

     Options:
       Yes
       No


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10 — Email (Almost Done)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q14. Email address
     Type: Email input
     Hint: "Required if you'd like to get involved or enter the raffle. We'll
           only use it to follow up — it won't be shared or connected to your
           survey answers."

     Required IF:  Respondent selected "Yes" to the raffle (Q13)
                   OR selected any involvement option other than "None of the
                   above" (Q12).
     Optional IF:  Respondent declined the raffle AND selected "None of the
                   above" for involvement.

     Notes:
     - If optional and left blank: proceed is allowed.
     - If optional and partially entered: must be a valid email format to proceed.
     - Inline validation error shown on blur if email is non-empty and invalid.
     - Respondents who provide an email receive an automated thank-you email
       upon submission.
     - Respondents who selected "Become a featured artist" receive a magic link
       to set up their artist profile.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 11 — One Last Thing (Referral)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q15. Where did you learn about Art Here?
     Type: Multi-select (checkboxes) — select all that apply
     Hint: "Select all that apply."
     Required: at least one option must be selected to submit.
     (This screen's Next button is the Submit button.)

     Options (first 4 randomized; "Other" pinned):
       Multnomah Arts Center  [randomized]
       A business in Multnomah Village  [randomized]
       Local art gallery  [randomized]
       Word of mouth  [randomized]
       Other  [pinned] → reveals text input

     Notes:
     - If Other text is entered, stored as "Other: [text]".
     - If Other is selected but no text entered, stored as "Other".

          Optional: —


================================================================================
AUTOMATED ACTIONS ON SUBMISSION
================================================================================

Two actions are triggered automatically when a survey is submitted:

1. Thank-You Email
   Sent to any respondent who provided an email address.
   From: hello@artishere.org
   Subject: "Thank you for completing the PDX Community Survey!"

2. Artist Profile Magic Link
   Sent when the respondent selected "Become a featured artist" AND provided
   an email. A user account and placeholder artist profile are created
   automatically. The magic link allows the artist to log in and complete their
   profile without a password.


================================================================================

================================================================================

Several multi-select questions randomize their option order on each page load
to prevent order bias. "Other," "None of the above," and similar catch-all
options are always pinned at the bottom.

Question              Randomized?           Pinned at Bottom
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Occupation (Q3)       First 10 options      Homemaker, Not currently working,
                                            Retired, Other, Prefer not to say
Portland Support (Q6) First 7 options       Other, None of the above
Recent Activities (Q9) First 7 options      Other, None of the above
Goals (Q10)           All options           Other
Referral (Q15)        First 4 options       Other
Get Involved (Q12)    None — fixed order    Other, None of the above
Artist Status (Q7)    None — fixed order    —
Familiarity (Q4)      None — fixed order    —
Career Stage (Q8)     None — fixed order    —
Raffle (Q13)          None — fixed order    —
